# Does low-stop routing avoid every traffic light?

No. [Low-stop routing](../concepts/low-stop-routing.md) prefers streets with fewer signals and keeps off highways and busy roads when a quieter alternative exists. You still read the layer marks and move a waypoint if a remaining light sits on a corner you cannot skip.
