# A Runner's Guide to Traffic Lights, Crossings, and Route Intelligence

Running route traffic light avoidance means reading signal and crossing marks, then asking for a quieter pedestrian line. [Run Without Stops](../index.md) does that with low-stop mode plus traffic-light, crossing, highway, and busy-road layers.

The marks are OpenStreetMap for the streets on screen, not a live countdown. Remaining lights on the line are real waits. See [low-stop routing](../concepts/low-stop-routing.md) and the parent [complete guide](the-complete-guide-to-building-a-run-without-stops.md).
